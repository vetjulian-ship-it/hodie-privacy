HODIE Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

HODIE respects your privacy.

HODIE is a personal productivity and daily reflection app designed to help you plan, organize, and reflect on your day.

1. Information We Collect

HODIE does not require an account, registration, or login.

HODIE does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The information you enter into the app, including plans, subtasks, timeline entries, and assessments, is stored locally on your device.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Your entries are used solely to provide the features of HODIE, including organizing your plans, managing your daily timeline, and reviewing your daily assessments.

HODIE does not transmit your entries to external servers.

3. Data Storage

Your entries are stored locally using your device's local storage.

Because your data is stored locally, HODIE does not have access to your entries.

If you clear the app's local data or delete the app, locally stored data may be deleted.

4. Third-Party Services

HODIE does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising services, or external data collection services.

5. Children's Privacy

HODIE does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the updated version of this policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided in the app's Developer section.
